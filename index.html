A NOTE ON LOOKING

To be here
is enough.

A shoulder against a window.
A chair with no one in it.
Light arriving without an invitation.

These photographs gather around the edges of ordinary life. In carriages, on pavements, beside water: people come into view, and move beyond it. The frame holds a little of what passes.
